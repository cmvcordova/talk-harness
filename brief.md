# Brief

Answer these before any slide exists. Short answers are fine. Leave a line
blank if you do not know, and Claude will ask. Everything Claude builds
follows from this file, so a wrong answer here costs more than anywhere else.

## Who is in the room

Who they are, what they already know, and what vocabulary they do not have.
"My lab" is not enough: say whether they know your method, your data, your
field's jargon.

>

## The one thing they leave with

One sentence. If they remember nothing else, they remember this.

>

## Length

Total minutes, and how many of them are for questions.

>

## Language

The language of the talk. Technical terms stay in English unless you say
otherwise.

>

## Date, venue, format

Date, place or video link, projector or screen share, and whether you can
see the room.

>

## What exists, and where

Every result, figure, table, dataset or paper the talk may draw on, with its
path on this machine or its URL. Claude uses nothing that is not listed here
or found from here. A number with no file becomes `TODO[value]` on the slide.

>

## What must not be said or shown

Unpublished numbers, names, a venue, a reviewer, a company. Anything under
embargo or review.

>

## What worked last time

What people said they liked about a previous talk of yours, and anything
they said did not land.

>

## Material to reuse

Previous decks, notes, blog posts, a paper's figures. Paths or URLs.

>

## Anything else

Constraints, a story you want to open with, a slide you already see.

>
